# Leave Management System - User Guide

## Overview

The Leave Management System is an integrated feature of the HSC Occupational Health System that automates compliance with Zimbabwe's HSB 3/2017 leave regulations. It tracks sick leave from the incident date, automatically calculates payment stages (Full Pay / Half Pay / Medical Discharge Eligible), and flags cases for medical board review at critical milestones (90 days and 180 days).

This guide covers workflows for HR staff, Medical Board members, and system administrators.
